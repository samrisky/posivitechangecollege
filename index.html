About Our School

Positive Change College is a leading secondary school dedicated to academic excellence, character formation, and high-stakes exam success. We support students from JSS1 through SS3 with a caring, disciplined learning environment.

Our school blends strong classroom instruction with practical guidance for WAEC and NECO preparation, leadership development, and community service. We are committed to helping every student reach their highest potential.

Located in Abuja, Nigeria · Serving students across the region
