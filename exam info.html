WAEC & NECO at Positive Change

Our secondary school offers structured exam preparation for Junior Secondary and Senior Secondary classes. Students receive guidance in subject registration, revision planning, mock exams, and official entry procedures.

Eligibility

All registered JSS3 and SS3 students who meet classroom attendance and continuous assessment standards are eligible to register for WAEC and NECO.

Exam Support

Our school provides revision lessons, practice tests, marking clinics, and subject mentoring in English, Mathematics, Sciences, and Arts.

Registration Checklist
  • Valid school ID card and birth certificate
  • Completed WAEC / NECO registration form
  • Correct subject combination approved by school counselor
  • Paid exam fees and necessary school charges
  • Signed parental authorization
Exam Timeline

6–8 Weeks Before

Confirm subject registration, collect exam notices, and begin weekly revision blocks.

4 Weeks Before

Take school mock exams, review past papers, and attend extra tutorial sessions.

2 Weeks Before

Complete final topic reviews, practice exam pacing, and meet with teachers about weak areas.

Exam Week

Arrive early with all materials, stay calm, follow instructions, and use good exam time management.

Exam Preparation Resources

Past Questions

Students practice with past WAEC and NECO papers to learn question formats and exam techniques.

Revision Classes

Extra classes are available after school and on weekends for core subjects and exam writing strategies.

Exam Counseling

Our guidance team helps students choose the right subject combination and manage exam stress.

Important Notes for Parents

Your support makes a big difference. Encourage attendance, provide a quiet revision space, and keep track of exam fees and school deadlines.

Key reminder: Exam details are published by WAEC and NECO each year. Positive Change College will share all official registration deadlines, exam schedules, and admission numbers with every student.
