Quick Answers
What is the application fee?
The application fee is $50 and is non-refundable. It can be paid online via credit card or bank transfer.
Are standardized tests required?
For the 2025 intake, SAT/ACT scores are optional. However, submitting strong scores may strengthen your application.
Can I visit the campus before applying?
Yes! We offer guided campus tours every weekday. You can book a tour through our admissions office.
Does the college offer scholarships?
Yes, we offer merit-based and need-based scholarships. The deadline to apply for scholarships is March 1st.
How do I transfer credits from another college?
You can submit your official transcripts for evaluation. Transfer credits are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.
